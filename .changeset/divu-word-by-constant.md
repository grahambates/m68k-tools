Add `optimization/divu-word-by-constant`, a conditional suggestion that replaces `divu.w #n,Dm` (up to 144 cycles) with a multiply by the reciprocal of `n`, for example `mulu.w #52429,d0 ; swap d0 ; lsr.w #3,d0` for a division by 10 (76 cycles), or a few shifts and adds for a small dividend. It only produces the quotient, so it is offered only when the register's upper word, where `divu` leaves the remainder, is provably unused, and only when N, Z, V and C are dead. Each recipe is exact only below a stated dividend, which cannot be proven from the code: the message says the range it assumes and the notes list cheaper recipes for smaller dividends. Recipes are generated by `scripts/generate-divide-recipes.mjs` from a reciprocal search and from `scripts/divide-shift-candidates.json` (found offline by `scripts/search-divide-shift-recipes.mjs`), each costed with 68kcounter and checked exhaustively over its range, and again against Musashi. The rule applies to 68000 targets only.
